Sunlit Earth

A free and open source desktop app that renders Earth as seen from space and sets it as your wallpaper. Your view of Earth updates throughout the day.

Features

  • 📸

    Your view

    Camera controls and presets let you choose your view of Earth.

  • 🌍

    The time of day

    Sunlight and city lights follow the current date and time, and the wallpaper updates throughout the day.

  • ❄️

    The seasons

    Detailed surface textures based on NASA imagery change with the seasons, sea ice included.

  • ⛅️

    The weather

    Recent satellite cloud imagery shows the changing weather.

  • 🌌

    The sky

    The Sun, Moon, planets, and stars are rendered at their astronomically correct positions.

  • 🖥️

    Your monitors

    Wallpapers fit your monitor layout, as mirrored views or as one continuous panorama.

  • 📦

    Works offline

    All contents are shipped with the app. Only recent cloud imagery need an internet connection.

  • ⚙️

    Everything adjustable

    Camera position, lighting, the atmosphere, and the size and brightness of the Sun, Moon and stars.

For x86-64.

With Scoop

scoop bucket add sunlit-earth https://github.com/sunlit-earth/scoop-bucket
scoop install sunlit-earth/sunlit-earth
First start

Unpack the zip file and run sunlit-earth.exe. On the first start, Windows might show a blue "Windows protected your PC" dialog with only a "Don't run" button. Click "More info", and a "Run anyway" button appears.

macOS

Apple silicon {{ files['macos-aarch64-app'].size }} Intel {{ files['macos-x86_64-app'].size }}

The app as a .zip. Not sure which one? About This Mac in the Apple menu shows an Apple M chip on Apple silicon.

With Homebrew

brew install --cask sunlit-earth/tap/sunlit-earth
First start

Unzip the download and move Sunlit Earth.app to Applications. The app is not notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS says it could not verify that the app is free of malware. To allow it, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Sunlit Earth, and confirm with your password if asked. Installing with Homebrew avoids all this.
